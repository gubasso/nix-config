# Everforest — Principles

- **Softness over punch.** Low contrast is the point; the accent green guides
  without shouting.
- **Backgrounds recede.** Muted dark green-grey base; warm cream foreground.
- **Semantic colors keep their jobs.** Red = error, orange = warn, green =
  success, blue = info.
- **Comfort for long sessions.** Values are chosen to reduce eye strain.
- **Calm motion.** No flashing or churn.

## Do

- Use `accent` (green) for selection, active borders, prompts.
- Layer chrome with `surface`/`overlay`.

## Don't

- Don't raise saturation to "pop" — that breaks the theme's character.
- Don't add hues outside the palette; extend the palette instead.
