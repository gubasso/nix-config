# Purple City — Identity

**Concept.** A neon-noir metropolis after midnight: near-black violet skies over
deep purple avenues, lit by magenta signage and cool blue window-glow.

**Mood.** Nocturnal, focused, a little cinematic. High contrast between an almost
black background and bright, saturated accents — made for long sessions in a dark
room.

**Polarity.** Dark.

**Signature.** Magenta (`base0E`, `#C89DEA`) is the accent: prompts, selections,
active borders. Backgrounds fall to true near-black (`#010005`) so the accents
read as light.

**Intended use.** The reference theme and the default "showcase" look for
purple-leaning hosts. Pairs with the `catppuccin` (mocha) nvim colorscheme for
editors that have their own polished dark theme.
